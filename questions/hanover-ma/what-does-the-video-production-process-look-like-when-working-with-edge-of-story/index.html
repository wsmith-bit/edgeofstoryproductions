Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films in Hanover

When engaging with Edge of Story Films for your video production needs in Hanover, MA, you can expect a structured and collaborative process tailored to your specific requirements. From initial contact to final delivery, here’s what you can anticipate in terms of steps, timelines, and what you need to be prepared for.

Phase 1: Initial Consultation

The journey begins with an initial consultation, where you’ll discuss your video goals, target audience, and the message you want to convey. This phase typically takes about 1 week. If you’re a law firm located near Hanover Junction, for example, you might express a need for a testimonial video that humanizes your attorneys. Prepare to share your vision and any existing materials that could assist in the creative process.

Phase 2: Concept Development

Following the consultation, Edge of Story Films will develop a creative concept that aligns with your objectives. This phase includes brainstorming ideas and outlining the video’s structure. It usually takes about 1-2 weeks. During this time, you may be asked to review storyboards or scripts, so it’s essential to maintain clear communication.

Phase 3: Production Planning

Once the concept is finalized, the production planning phase begins. This includes scheduling shoot dates, scouting locations (which might involve local sites in Hanover, like the Queen Elizabeth II September 11th Garden), and organizing the necessary equipment. Expect this phase to take another 1-2 weeks, depending on the complexity of your project and the availability of your team, especially if you have multiple stakeholders involved.

Phase 4: Filming

The actual filming can typically be completed within 1-3 days, but this can vary based on the length and complexity of your video. Edge of Story Films utilizes top-notch equipment, shooting in 4K and 6K to ensure high-quality results. If your team includes camera-shy attorneys, rest assured that the team will work to make them comfortable on camera during this phase. Make sure you have all necessary approvals in place to keep the filming on schedule.

Phase 5: Post-Production

After filming, the post-production phase begins. This includes video editing, adding motion graphics, and incorporating any necessary animations. Typically, this stage can take 2-4 weeks, depending on the project’s complexity and your feedback speed. Clients often find this part exciting as the concept comes to life, but it’s crucial to provide timely feedback to avoid delays.

Phase 6: Final Review and Delivery

Once the video is edited, you’ll receive a draft for review. You’ll have the opportunity to suggest any changes or adjustments. After final approvals, Edge of Story Films will deliver the finished product. This final delivery phase can take an additional week. The complete timeline from start to finish can range from 6 to 12 weeks, depending on various factors such as scheduling and the complexity of the video.

  • Changes in project scope mid-production.
