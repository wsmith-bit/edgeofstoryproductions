Understanding the Video Production Process for Corporate Videos in Concord

When you're looking to create a corporate video in Concord, partnering with a professional video production company like Edge of Story Films ensures a seamless and effective process. From the initial consultation to the final media delivery, here’s what you can expect at each stage of the production process.

1. Initial Consultation (1-2 Weeks)

The first step is to schedule an initial consultation with Edge of Story Films. This meeting is crucial for understanding your specific needs, goals, and the message you want to convey. Given Concord's unique demographic—where the median household income is around $96,505 and many local businesses are focused on professional services—it's essential to communicate how your corporate video will resonate with your target audience.

2. Concept Development (2-3 Weeks)

Once your goals are established, the team will move on to the creative concept development. This includes brainstorming ideas that align with your brand and objectives. During this phase, you’ll review storyboards and scripts, ensuring that the video's tone and messaging reflect your company's identity. Delays can occur if feedback is slow or if additional revisions are required, so maintaining open communication is key.

3. Pre-Production Planning (1-2 Weeks)

After finalizing the concept, the pre-production phase begins. This includes casting, location scouting, and scheduling. In Concord, you may want to showcase local landmarks such as the Concord Museum or the picturesque West Concord Park to create a relatable backdrop for your video. Be prepared to discuss logistics, including the availability of team members and any necessary permits for shooting in public spaces.

4. Production (1-3 Days)

The production phase is where the magic happens. Edge of Story Films will handle all aspects of filming, utilizing 4K and 6K high-definition technology to capture stunning visuals. Depending on the complexity of your video, this phase can take anywhere from one to three days. Make sure your team is available and ready to shoot as delays during this phase can affect the overall timeline.

5. Post-Production (2-4 Weeks)

Once filming is complete, the post-production phase begins. This is where the editing, motion graphics, and any necessary animations come into play. Typically, this phase takes two to four weeks, depending on the complexity of the video and the number of revisions needed. Providing timely feedback during this stage can help expedite the process.

6. Final Delivery (1 Week)

After the final edits are made, you'll receive your completed video for review. Edge of Story Films will make any last-minute adjustments as requested. Once approved, the final media will be delivered to you in the desired formats. Expect this final step to take about a week.

Summary

  • Initial Consultation: 1-2 Weeks
  • Concept Development: 2-3 Weeks
  • Pre-Production Planning: 1-2 Weeks
  • Production: 1-3 Days
  • Post-Production: 2-4 Weeks
  • Final Delivery: 1 Week

By understanding this process and maintaining clear communication, you can ensure a smooth experience that results in a high-quality corporate video aimed at enhancing your brand's credibility in Concord's competitive market.
