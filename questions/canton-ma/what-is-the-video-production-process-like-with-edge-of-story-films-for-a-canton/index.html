Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

If you're a business in Canton, Massachusetts looking to create impactful video content, understanding the process at Edge of Story Films can help you anticipate what to expect from start to finish. The company specializes in end-to-end video production services, including corporate videos, testimonial videos, and animated explainers, tailored specifically to meet the needs of businesses like yours.

Initial Consultation

The journey begins with an initial consultation. During this phase, you’ll discuss your vision, goals, and the specific message you want to convey through your video. This is a crucial step where you articulate your objectives, whether it's to humanize your brand, explain complex services, or drive engagement through social media. Expect this meeting to take place either in person, if convenient, or virtually, accommodating your busy schedule.

Planning and Pre-Production

Once the initial consultation is complete, the next phase is planning and pre-production. This stage typically lasts about 1-2 weeks. Edge of Story Films will help you develop a creative concept, create a script, and plan the logistics of the shoot. You’ll collaborate closely with their team to finalize your ideas, ensuring they align with your brand and target audience. Be prepared to provide feedback and make decisions on elements like locations, talent, and the style of the video.

Production Phase

Production is where the magic happens! Depending on the complexity of your video, this stage can take anywhere from 1-5 days. Edge of Story Films employs a professional crew equipped with high-definition cameras to capture your vision. If you’re in Canton, filming may take place at local landmarks, like the Canton Historical Museum, to enhance the local appeal. During this time, you can expect to be hands-on, especially if your video includes interviews or testimonials from your team.

Post-Production

Once filming is completed, the project moves to the post-production phase, which can take about 2-4 weeks. Here, the footage is edited, and elements like motion graphics and sound design are added to create a polished final product. Edge of Story Films takes great care to ensure that the video meets your expectations and resonates with your target audience. You’ll have opportunities to review drafts and provide feedback before the final delivery.

Final Delivery and Distribution

After revisions have been made and you’ve approved the final cut, your video will be delivered in the desired formats suitable for your website, social media, or other platforms. The entire process, from initial consultation to final delivery, typically spans about 6-10 weeks, depending on factors like scheduling and complexity of the project.

What Can Cause Delays?

  • Scheduling Conflicts: Coordinating schedules can be challenging, especially if multiple stakeholders are involved.
  • Feedback Loops: The review process may take longer if feedback is not provided promptly.
  • Complex Projects: The more intricate your video, the longer it may take to perfect.
