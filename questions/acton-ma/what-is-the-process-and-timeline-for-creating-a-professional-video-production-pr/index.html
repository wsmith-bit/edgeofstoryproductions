Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

For law firms in Acton seeking to establish a strong online presence through professional video content, partnering with Edge of Story Films can be a transformative decision. The process of creating a video project typically unfolds in several key phases, ensuring that your firm’s unique story is effectively communicated to prospective clients.

Phase 1: Initial Consultation (1–2 Weeks)

The journey begins with an initial consultation. During this meeting, you’ll discuss your firm’s goals, target audience, and the specific type of videos you need, such as attorney intro videos or client testimonials. Edge of Story Films will guide you through their offerings and help you identify the best approach to meet your needs. This phase typically takes 1 to 2 weeks, depending on your availability and scheduling.

Phase 2: Creative Development (1–3 Weeks)

Once you’ve outlined your needs, the next step is creative development. Edge of Story Films will present concepts and storyboards tailored to your law firm’s identity. This phase can take anywhere from 1 to 3 weeks, as it involves refining ideas and ensuring that the vision aligns with your firm’s branding. Collaborating closely with the team helps to avoid any delays and ensures a smoother creative process.

Phase 3: Filming (1 Week)

After finalizing the creative concepts, the filming phase begins. Depending on the complexity of the project, filming typically takes about a week. For law firms like yours in Acton, this might include capturing footage at your office or a local landmark to contextualize your services within the community. It’s essential to have attorneys available for shooting during this time, so scheduling is crucial. If your attorneys are camera-shy, Edge of Story Films offers coaching to help them feel more comfortable in front of the camera.

Phase 4: Post-Production (2–4 Weeks)

Following filming, the post-production phase begins, which involves editing, adding motion graphics, and finalizing the video content. This stage can take 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the complexity of the edits and any revisions needed. Edge of Story Films prides itself on high-quality production, so attention to detail is key here. You’ll have opportunities to provide feedback and request adjustments to ensure the final product aligns perfectly with your vision.

Phase 5: Final Delivery (1 Week)

The final phase is delivery, where you’ll receive the completed video in various formats for your website and social media platforms. This phase typically takes about a week, allowing time for any last-minute adjustments or formats you may need. Expect to discuss the best strategies for utilizing the video content effectively in your marketing efforts.

What to Expect Throughout the Process

  • Effective Communication: Regular check-ins with the Edge of Story Films team will help keep your project on track.
  • Flexibility: Be prepared for potential scheduling conflicts, especially if you have multiple attorneys involved in the project.
  • Clear Timelines: Each phase will have clear deadlines, but factors like attorney availability or the complexity of edits can influence the timeline.

By understanding this process, you can ensure that your law firm in Acton is well-prepared to create impactful video content that builds credibility and trust with prospective clients.
