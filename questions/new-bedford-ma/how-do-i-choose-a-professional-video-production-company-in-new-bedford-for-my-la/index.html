Choosing the Right Video Production Company in New Bedford

When it comes to selecting a professional video production company for your law firm in New Bedford, it’s essential to focus on credentials, experience, and the specific needs of your practice. High-quality video content can significantly enhance your firm’s credibility and help potential clients connect with your attorneys on a personal level. Here’s what to consider.

1. Check Credentials and Industry Experience

  • Professional Affiliations: Look for companies like Edge of Story Films, which have experience working with legal professionals. They understand the nuances of creating content that resonates with potential clients.
  • Portfolio of Work: Review their previous projects to gauge the quality of their work. A strong portfolio will showcase a range of video types, including attorney intro videos and client testimonials that effectively communicate your firm’s values and services.

2. Evaluate Their Understanding of the Legal Sector

It’s essential that the video production team understands the specific challenges faced by law firms, such as compliance considerations and the need for professionalism in content. Ask about their experience in producing legal videos, and whether they can demonstrate a clear understanding of your practice areas, such as personal injury or family law.

3. Questions to Ask on Your First Call

  • What experience do you have working with law firms in New Bedford?
  • Can you provide case studies or testimonials from previous law firm clients?
  • How do you approach scripting and storyboarding for our specific legal services?
  • What is your process for ensuring compliance with legal advertising regulations?
  • What is your typical turnaround time for video production?

4. Red Flags to Watch For

  • Lack of Legal Experience: If the company has no previous experience working with law firms, they may not fully understand the industry’s unique challenges.
  • Poor Communication: Pay attention to how they communicate during initial discussions. If they struggle to explain their process or don’t seem responsive, it could reflect their level of service.
  • Generic Portfolio: A portfolio filled with generic content that lacks depth or creativity may indicate that they do not tailor their services to meet your needs.

5. Local Context Matters

New Bedford’s vibrant legal market, combined with the city’s rich history and community engagement, creates a unique backdrop for your video content. As the city continues to grow, with a population surpassing 100,000, it’s vital to have a professional video that sets your firm apart from competitors. Look for a production company that can help you convey your firm’s story authentically, whether through video content for local events or promotional materials aimed at potential clients.
