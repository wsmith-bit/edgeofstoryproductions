Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

If you're a business in Maynard looking to develop impactful video content, understanding the production process at Edge of Story Films can help streamline your experience. Their approach includes several key phases that ensure your vision is realized while meeting your business objectives effectively.

1. Initial Consultation

The journey begins with a consultation where you discuss your project needs, goals, and vision. This step usually lasts about a week as you’ll collaborate with the team to clarify your objectives, whether that’s creating an animated explainer video or a series of testimonials. The goal here is to establish a solid foundation for your project, ensuring that Edge of Story Films captures the essence of your business, especially in the context of Maynard's unique market and demographics.

2. Creative Concept Development

After the initial consultation, the team at Edge of Story Films will move into the creative concept phase. This typically takes about 2–3 weeks. They will develop a script and storyboard, tailored to resonate with your target audience in Maynard and the Greater Boston area. 3. Production Planning

Next is production planning, which involves logistics such as location scouting in Maynard, scheduling shoots, and coordinating with any necessary personnel. 4. Filming

Once everything is in place, the filming phase will commence. Depending on the complexity of the project, this can take anywhere from 1 to several days. 5. Post-Production

After filming is complete, the post-production phase begins. This step typically lasts 3–4 weeks and involves editing, motion graphics, and animation as needed. You'll have the opportunity to review drafts and provide feedback, ensuring the final product aligns with your expectations and goals. The final step is the delivery of your completed video.
