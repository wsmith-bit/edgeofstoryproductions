Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

If you're a business in Methuen looking to enhance your online presence with professional video content, understanding the video production process with Edge of Story Films can help you set realistic expectations. The process is designed to be comprehensive, ensuring that you receive high-quality video tailored to your specific needs, whether it’s a website video, testimonial, or marketing content.

Step 1: Initial Consultation

The journey begins with an initial consultation. This is where we discuss your business goals, target audience, and the type of video content you need. For example, if you are a growing law firm in Methuen looking to establish credibility, we’ll explore options like attorney introduction videos or client testimonials. This phase typically takes about one week to schedule and conduct.

Step 2: Creative Concept Development

Once we understand your vision, we move to the creative concept development phase. Here, our team will brainstorm ideas and create a storyboard that aligns with your branding and messaging. This phase can take anywhere from 1-2 weeks, depending on your feedback and any necessary revisions. Effective communication during this stage is crucial to ensure we are on the right path.

Step 3: Pre-Production Planning

Following concept approval, we enter the pre-production planning stage. This involves scheduling shoot dates, securing locations (which can include notable spots in Methuen like the Methuen Historical Society), casting if needed, and finalizing the script. This phase can take about 1-3 weeks, depending on the complexity of the shoot and availability of participants.

Step 4: Production

Next comes the production phase, where the actual filming takes place. Our team will handle everything from lighting to sound. For instance, if you’re shooting a testimonial video at your Methuen office, we’ll ensure the environment reflects your brand’s identity. This phase typically lasts 1-3 days, depending on the number of videos being produced.

Step 5: Post-Production

After filming, we move to post-production, which includes editing, motion graphics, and adding animations if necessary. This is where your video starts to take shape. The post-production phase usually requires about 2-4 weeks to ensure the final product meets our high standards and your expectations.

Step 6: Review and Final Delivery

Once the editing is complete, we’ll share the draft with you for review. This is your opportunity to request any changes or adjustments. After your final approval, we’ll deliver the finished video in the desired format, ready for you to use on your website or social media channels. This final step can take about 1 week.

What Can Cause Delays?

While we strive for a smooth process, several factors can potentially delay production, such as:

  • Unavailability of key stakeholders during the review process
  • Weather conditions affecting outdoor shoots
  • Revisions that require significant changes to the initial concept

Ensuring a Smooth Experience

To ensure a smooth experience, we recommend maintaining open communication and being proactive in scheduling meetings and reviews. By doing so, you can help us stick to the timeline and achieve your video production goals effectively.
