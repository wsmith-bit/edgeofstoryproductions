1. Check Credentials and Experience

  • Industry Specialization: Look for companies that have specific experience producing videos for law firms. Edge of Story Films, for instance, specializes in attorney intro videos, client testimonials, and case update explainers, which can help humanize your attorneys and build credibility.
  • Past Work: Review portfolios to assess the quality and style of previous projects. A strong portfolio should showcase a range of video types, including website videos and promotional materials, demonstrating their ability to adapt to different needs.

2. Ask the Right Questions

When you first reach out to a video production company, consider asking the following questions:

  • What is your process for understanding a law firm’s unique value proposition?
  • Can you provide case studies or testimonials from previous law firm clients?
  • How do you ensure compliance with legal advertising regulations?
  • What is your turnaround time for video production?

3. Look for Red Flags

As you evaluate potential providers, be mindful of any red flags that might indicate a poor fit:

  • Lack of Relevant Experience: If a company primarily works in unrelated industries, they may not understand the nuances of legal marketing.
  • Poor Communication: If they are slow to respond to inquiries or vague in their answers, this could indicate potential issues in collaboration later on.
  • Generic Content: Avoid providers who offer cookie-cutter solutions without considering your firm’s specific needs.
