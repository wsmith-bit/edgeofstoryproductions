Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

If you're a law firm in Weymouth looking to enhance your online presence and build trust with prospective clients, understanding the video production process with Edge of Story Films is essential. This professional video production company specializes in creating compelling content tailored specifically for legal practices, helping you communicate your services effectively. Here's a detailed breakdown of what you can expect when you collaborate with them.

Phase 1: Initial Consultation

The journey begins with an initial consultation. During this phase, you'll discuss your specific needs, goals, and the type of videos you require, such as attorney intro videos or client testimonials. For a law firm situated in Weymouth, this might include exploring how to humanize your attorneys and create a compelling narrative that resonates with the local community. This meeting typically lasts about an hour and can occur via phone or in-person at their Wakefield office.

Phase 2: Concept Development

After the initial meeting, Edge of Story Films will move into the concept development stage. Here, they will draft a creative brief based on your input, outlining the video’s objectives, style, and messaging. This process can take about one to two weeks, depending on how quickly you provide feedback. It’s crucial to ensure that your firm’s unique attributes are highlighted, especially given Weymouth's competitive legal landscape.

Phase 3: Production Planning

Once the concept is approved, the next step involves detailed production planning. This includes scheduling filming dates, selecting locations, and casting any necessary actors or on-screen talent, such as your attorneys. Given the busy schedules of law firm partners, this phase often requires coordination that could take another week or two. Edge of Story Films is adept at accommodating your team’s availability, especially for firms that may be located near the Weymouth Town Beach or other recognizable local landmarks.

Phase 4: Filming

Filming typically occurs over one to three days, depending on the complexity of the project. Edge of Story Films utilizes state-of-the-art 4K and 6K cameras to capture high-quality footage. They’ll direct your attorneys to ensure they feel comfortable on camera, addressing any nervousness they might have. This is a pivotal step to ensure that the final product doesn’t feel stiff or unnatural, which is a common concern among law firms.

Phase 5: Post-Production

Following filming, the project transitions into post-production. During this phase, the footage is edited, and any necessary motion graphics or animations are added. This process can take about three to four weeks, depending on the project's complexity. Edge of Story Films places a strong emphasis on ensuring that the final video aligns with your branding and effectively communicates your message. They also conduct reviews with you to ensure satisfaction before the final media is delivered.

Phase 6: Final Delivery and Strategy Implementation

Once the editing is complete, the final videos are delivered in the formats you need for your website and social media platforms. This is a crucial step for law firms in Weymouth looking to establish credibility online and attract new clients. Edge of Story Films also provides guidance on how to implement your videos effectively, whether on your website or through targeted digital marketing campaigns.

Potential Delays and Smooth Experiences

  • Delays: Common delays might arise from scheduling conflicts or extended feedback cycles, particularly if multiple stakeholders are involved in the decision-making process.
  • Smooth Experience: To ensure a seamless process, being prepared with your goals and feedback can significantly streamline each phase.
