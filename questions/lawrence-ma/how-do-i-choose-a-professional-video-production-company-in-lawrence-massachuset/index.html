How to Choose a Professional Video Production Company in Lawrence, Massachusetts

If you're a business in Lawrence looking to elevate your brand through professional video content, choosing the right video production company is crucial. With a high density of mid-sized companies in the area, many have outgrown DIY video solutions and are seeking specialized expertise. Here’s how to evaluate your options effectively.

1. Check Credentials and Experience

When vetting video production companies, start by assessing their credentials. Look for companies that have a solid history of producing high-quality video content tailored for businesses similar to yours. For instance, Edge of Story Films, located just outside of Lawrence in Wakefield, is known for its end-to-end video production services, which include corporate videos, promotional content, and more. Check if they have any industry accolades, such as awards or nominations, like their recent New England Emmy nomination.

2. Review Their Portfolio

A strong portfolio is a telling signal of a company’s capabilities. Look for a variety of styles and types of video content. Pay attention to whether their previous work resonates with your brand’s identity and the quality of production. Edge of Story Films showcases a range of services, from animated explainer videos to client testimonials, which can be a good indicator of their versatility and expertise.

3. Ask the Right Questions

  • What is your production process? Understanding their workflow is essential. A company that manages all stages—concept development, filming, editing, and delivery—can offer a more cohesive final product.
  • Can you provide case studies or references? Ask for specific examples of how their videos have helped other clients achieve measurable results.
  • How do you handle compliance and approvals? This is especially important for sectors like finance, where compliance constraints can slow down production.
  • What is your approach to coaching on-camera talent? If your team is camera-shy, understanding how they work with clients to help them feel comfortable is vital.

4. Look for Red Flags

As you evaluate potential providers, keep an eye out for red flags that could indicate a lack of professionalism or expertise. These can include:

  • Inconsistent quality in their portfolio.
  • A lack of transparency about pricing and the production process.
  • Negative reviews or feedback from past clients, especially regarding their ability to meet deadlines.
  • Failure to understand your specific needs or reluctance to customize their services.

5. Local Knowledge and Understanding

Given Lawrence’s unique local context—home to various industries including manufacturing and technology—it's beneficial to choose a production company that understands the local market. Edge of Story Films is familiar with the needs of local businesses and can tailor their services accordingly.

Conclusion

Choosing the right video production company in Lawrence requires careful consideration of credentials, portfolio, and communication. By asking the right questions and being mindful of potential red flags, you can make a well-informed decision.
