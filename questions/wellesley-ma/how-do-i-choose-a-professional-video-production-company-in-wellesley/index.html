Portfolio Signals

  • Diverse Offerings: A robust portfolio should demonstrate a range of video types, including corporate videos, testimonial videos, and website videos. Look for examples that show versatility and creativity in storytelling.

Questions to Ask on the First Call

  • What is your production process, from conceptualization to final delivery?
  • Can you provide case studies or examples of past work similar to my project?
  • How do you handle revisions and feedback throughout the project?
  • What is your typical turnaround time for projects of this nature?

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Lack of Transparency: If a company is unwilling to share details about their process or past work, this could indicate a lack of professionalism.

  • Poor Communication: If you find it difficult to get answers to your questions during initial conversations, this could foreshadow future challenges during the project.
