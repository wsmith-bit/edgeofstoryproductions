Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

If you're a business owner in Shrewsbury looking to elevate your brand through professional video content, understanding the process and timeline with Edge of Story Films can help clarify what to expect. From initial contact to final delivery, here's a breakdown of the key phases involved in creating a video tailored to your specific needs.

1. Initial Consultation (1-2 weeks)

The process begins with an initial consultation, where you will discuss your vision for the video. This is a vital step, as it allows you to convey your goals—whether it's creating an about-us video, client testimonials, or a corporate promotional video. During this meeting, you will also outline any specific messages or themes you want to emphasize. In Shrewsbury, where the business community is thriving, this stage is essential in identifying how your video can stand out among local competitors, particularly in professional sectors like law or finance.

2. Creative Concept Development (2-3 weeks)

After the initial consultation, the Edge of Story team will take time to develop a creative concept that aligns with your objectives. This phase includes script writing, storyboarding, and planning the logistics of the shoot. Given Shrewsbury's rich local identity and landmarks, like the Shrewsbury Museum, the team may suggest incorporating local elements that resonate with your audience. Expect to provide input and feedback during this stage to ensure the concept meets your expectations.

3. Filming (1-2 days)

The filming phase typically lasts one to two days, depending on the complexity of your video. For businesses in Shrewsbury, this could mean filming at your office or at various local sites. Edge of Story Films uses state-of-the-art equipment to capture high-definition footage, ensuring a polished final product. However, it’s important to schedule these days considering the availability of key personnel, such as attorneys or executives who may need to be on camera.

4. Editing and Post-Production (2-4 weeks)

Once filming is complete, your project will move into the editing phase. This involves cutting the footage, adding motion graphics and animations, and refining the sound. Typically, this phase takes two to four weeks. During this time, you will receive updates and have opportunities to provide feedback. The collaborative nature of this phase is crucial, especially for firms in competitive sectors like financial services in Shrewsbury, where the final video must clearly communicate complex information in an engaging way.

5. Final Review and Delivery (1 week)

After editing, you will receive a draft of the video for your review. This is your chance to make any final tweaks or adjustments. Following your approval, Edge of Story Films will deliver the final video in your preferred format, ready for use on your website, social media, or business presentations. This step usually takes about a week, ensuring that the final product is polished and aligned with your brand standards.

Potential Delays

  • Scheduling conflicts with key personnel during filming.
  • Delays in feedback during the editing stage.
  • Compliance reviews, especially for financial services firms.

A Smooth Experience

To ensure a smooth experience, maintain clear communication with the Edge of Story team and provide timely feedback at each stage. This approach will help keep the project on track and allow you to meet your marketing goals effectively.
