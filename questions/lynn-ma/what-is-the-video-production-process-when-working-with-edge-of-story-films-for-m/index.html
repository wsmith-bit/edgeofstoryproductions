Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

If you're a law firm in Lynn looking to enhance your online presence with professional video content, understanding the process of working with Edge of Story Films is essential. This boutique video production company specializes in creating tailored videos that build credibility and trust with prospective clients. Here’s a detailed breakdown of what to expect from first contact through to the final delivery of your video.

Step 1: Initial Consultation

The journey begins with an initial consultation, which can typically be scheduled within a week. During this meeting, you'll discuss your specific goals, whether it’s creating an attorney intro video, client testimonials, or website videos. This is also an opportunity to express any concerns, such as how your attorneys feel about being on camera, which is a common issue for many firms in Lynn.

Step 2: Concept Development

Following the consultation, the Edge of Story team will develop a creative concept tailored to your firm’s unique needs. This phase typically lasts 1-2 weeks, depending on your feedback and any needed revisions. If there are specific themes or messages you want to convey, make sure to communicate these clearly. The goal here is to ensure that the final product resonates well with your target audience in Lynn and reflects your firm's values.

Step 3: Pre-Production Planning

Once the concept is approved, the pre-production phase begins. This includes scriptwriting, storyboarding, and scheduling the shoot. Given that attorneys often have tight schedules, it's crucial to coordinate filming days well in advance. This phase could take another 1-2 weeks, and any delays in scheduling can extend the timeline.

Step 4: Production

Production involves the actual filming, which typically occurs over 1-3 days, depending on the complexity and number of videos being produced. Edge of Story Films utilizes high-definition 4K and 6K cameras to ensure the highest quality. Locations may include your law firm’s office in Downtown Lynn or even local landmarks like the Lynn Heritage State Park, which can add a unique touch to your video.

Step 5: Post-Production

After filming, the editing process begins. This phase can take 2-4 weeks, as it involves cutting footage, adding graphics, and ensuring that the final product aligns with your vision. The team will send you drafts for feedback, allowing you to request any changes or adjustments. Efficient communication during this phase is key to keeping the project on track.

Step 6: Final Delivery

After finalizing the edits, the completed video will be delivered to you in the agreed-upon formats. You can expect to receive your videos approximately 6-10 weeks after the initial consultation, depending on the complexity of the project and your timely feedback. A smooth experience hinges on clear communication and timely responses throughout the process.

Conclusion

Working with Edge of Story Films can significantly elevate your law firm’s online presence in Lynn. The entire process, from conceptualization to final delivery, is designed to be collaborative and efficient.
