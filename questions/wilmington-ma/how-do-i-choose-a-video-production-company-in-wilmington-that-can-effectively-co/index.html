Choosing the Right Video Production Company in Wilmington

When searching for a video production company in Wilmington to communicate your law firm's message, it’s essential to evaluate potential providers carefully. With an increasing number of businesses relocating from higher-cost areas like Arlington to Wilmington, the demand for local expertise in video production is growing. Here are some key criteria to consider when making your choice.

1. Credentials and Industry Experience

  • Check for Established Credentials: Look for companies with a solid foundation in video production. Edge of Story Films, based in nearby Wakefield, has a proven track record and has been nominated for a New England Emmy, which indicates a high standard of quality and recognition within the industry.
  • Relevant Experience with Legal Firms: Ensure the company has experience working specifically with law firms. They should understand the nuances of your practice areas, such as personal injury or corporate law, and how to present these topics effectively on camera.

2. Portfolio and Previous Work

Review their portfolio for examples of previous work:

  • Quality of Production: Look for high-definition video quality (4K or 6K) and engaging storytelling. A portfolio that showcases various types of videos—like client testimonials and attorney introduction videos—can demonstrate versatility.
  • Client Testimonials: Seek out reviews and testimonials from previous clients, especially from law firms or similar professional services. This feedback can provide insight into their ability to deliver results.

3. Questions to Ask on the First Call

When you first contact a video production company, consider asking the following questions:

  • What is your process for creating videos tailored to the legal industry?
  • Can you provide case studies or examples of measurable outcomes achieved for other law firms?
  • How do you handle client input during the production process?
  • What is your turnaround time for producing videos?

4. Red Flags to Watch For

While evaluating potential providers in Wilmington, keep an eye out for these warning signs:

  • Lack of Specific Experience: If a company does not have previous experience with law firms or similar professional services, they may struggle to understand your specific needs.
  • Generic Solutions: Be cautious of companies that offer one-size-fits-all solutions without a clear strategy tailored to your law firm's unique message and audience.
  • Poor Communication: If a provider is slow to respond or unclear in their explanations during initial discussions, that may be indicative of their overall service level.
