Understanding the Video Production Process for Law Firms in Wilmington

If you're a law firm in Wilmington considering video production services, you may be wondering about the specific steps involved when working with Edge of Story Films. This professional video production company, based in nearby Wakefield, specializes in creating high-quality video content tailored to the legal industry. Here's a detailed look at what you can expect throughout the production process.

1. Initial Consultation

The process begins with an initial consultation, where you’ll discuss your firm’s goals, target audience, and the specific type of video content you need—be it an attorney introduction video, client testimonials, or case update explainers. This stage typically lasts about one week, allowing you to gather your internal team and define your objectives. Edge of Story Films will guide you in outlining your vision and needs, ensuring that your firm’s unique identity is captured in the footage.

2. Creative Concept Development

Once your goals are clearly defined, the next phase is creative concept development. During this time, Edge of Story Films will present ideas and storyboards for your video. This phase usually takes about two weeks, depending on your feedback and revisions. It’s essential to ensure that the concept aligns with your firm’s branding and messaging, especially in a competitive market like Wilmington, where establishing credibility is crucial.

3. Production Planning

Following approval of the concept, the production planning phase begins. This involves scheduling filming dates and locations, which can be particularly important if you want to shoot in notable spots around Wilmington, such as the Wilmington Town Park or the Town Museum. This phase often takes one week and requires coordination with your staff, especially if they will be appearing on camera. It’s worth noting that scheduling may take longer if attorneys have tight calendars.

4. Filming

Filming typically occurs over one to three days, depending on the complexity and number of videos being produced. Edge of Story Films utilizes high-definition 4K and 6K video technology to ensure a polished look that feels authentic and professional. During filming, the Edge of Story team will coach your attorneys to help them feel comfortable on camera, addressing any nervousness and ensuring that the final product showcases their expertise effectively.

5. Post-Production

After filming, the post-production phase begins, lasting approximately three to four weeks. This stage includes editing, adding motion graphics, and producing any animated sequences that may be part of your video. Edge of Story Films will provide you with review drafts, allowing you to give feedback and make adjustments before the final delivery. This interaction is vital for ensuring that the final cut meets your expectations and adheres to the legal standards required.

6. Final Delivery

Once the edits are complete and approved, you will receive the final versions of your videos in the desired formats for online use, presentations, or social media. This is when your firm can start leveraging the new content to enhance your online presence and attract qualified leads. The entire process from initial consultation to final delivery can take around 8 to 12 weeks, depending on your responsiveness and the complexity of the project.

Conclusion

By understanding the video production process with Edge of Story Films, your Wilmington law firm can ensure a smooth and efficient experience. This structured approach not only saves time but also helps in producing high-quality video content that resonates with your target audience.
