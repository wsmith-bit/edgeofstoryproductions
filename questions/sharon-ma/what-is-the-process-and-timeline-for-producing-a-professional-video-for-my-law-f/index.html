Understanding the Video Production Process for Law Firms in Sharon

If you're a law firm in Sharon looking to produce a professional video, you might be wondering about the steps involved and how long the process will take. Edge of Story Films specializes in creating high-quality videos tailored to legal practices, and we can walk you through the entire process. Typically, you can expect the project to unfold over several key phases, each requiring your input and collaboration.

Phase 1: Initial Consultation (1-2 Weeks)

The journey begins with an initial consultation. During this phase, you’ll meet with our team to discuss your goals, target audience, and the specific message you want to convey through the video. This is your opportunity to express any concerns, such as previous experiences with video that felt stiff or unengaging. We’ll also help you understand the types of videos that work best for law firms, such as attorney intro videos or client testimonials. This phase typically takes about one to two weeks, depending on your schedule and availability.

Phase 2: Creative Concept Development (2-3 Weeks)

Once we have a clear understanding of your vision, our team will move into the creative concept development phase. This includes scriptwriting, storyboarding, and planning the filming logistics. We’ll collaborate closely with you to ensure that the content aligns with your firm's branding and messaging. This phase usually takes around two to three weeks, contingent on the number of revisions and feedback loops required.

Phase 3: Filming (1 Week)

With the creative direction finalized, we’ll schedule the shoot. Filming typically takes about one week, during which we’ll capture the footage needed for your video. We understand that attorneys can be time-constrained, so we’ll work around your schedule to make this as seamless as possible. Expect to allocate time for interviews and B-roll footage, which may include shots of your office in Sharon's Park Historic District or interactions with clients.

Phase 4: Editing and Post-Production (2-4 Weeks)

After filming, we move into the editing process. This is where the magic happens. Our team will edit the footage, add motion graphics, and incorporate any needed animations. Depending on the complexity of your video, this phase can take anywhere from two to four weeks. If you have specific compliance considerations, especially in the legal sector, we will ensure that all content adheres to industry standards.

Phase 5: Final Review and Delivery (1-2 Weeks)

The final phase involves a review of the completed video. You’ll have the opportunity to provide feedback and request final adjustments. Once approved, we will deliver the video in your desired format, ready for use on your website or social media platforms. This phase usually takes an additional one to two weeks, depending on the number of revisions required.

Potential Delays

  • Complexity of the content: If your message requires more detailed explanations or creative elements, it may extend the timeline.
  • Scheduling conflicts: Coordinating with busy attorneys can sometimes delay the filming phase.
  • Feedback loops: A lengthy review process can also stretch the timeline.

Smooth Experience

A smooth experience often comes from clear communication and timely feedback at each stage. Being proactive about scheduling and providing feedback will help keep the project on track.

In conclusion, producing a professional video for your law firm in Sharon typically spans several weeks, from consultation to delivery.
