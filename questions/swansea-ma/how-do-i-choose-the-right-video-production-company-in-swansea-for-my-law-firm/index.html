  • Portfolio Quality: Review their portfolio to assess the quality of the videos they produce. Are the videos polished and professional? Do they utilize high-definition formats, such as 4K or 6K? Companies like Edge of Story Films pride themselves on their high production values, which are essential in maintaining the professionalism of your law firm.
  • Understanding of Legal Nuances: It's vital that the production team understands the legal industry’s specific requirements, including compliance constraints and the need for an authoritative tone. Ask potential providers how they navigate these challenges, and look for examples where they successfully communicated complex legal concepts in an engaging manner.
  • Questions to Ask: During your first call, inquire about their process for developing video content. Here are some questions to consider:
    • What is your approach to understanding our firm's brand and values?
    • Can you provide examples of how you have helped other law firms increase their online presence through video?
    • What is your timeline for production and delivery?
    • How do you handle revisions and feedback?
  • Red Flags to Watch For: Be cautious of companies that guarantee results without showcasing past success stories, or those that seem unfamiliar with the legal industry. Also, if their previous work appears generic or lacks depth, it might be a sign that they won't be able to effectively communicate your firm's unique value proposition.
