Understanding the Corporate Video Production Process in South Weymouth

If you're a business in South Weymouth looking to create a corporate video, understanding the process and timeline with Edge of Story Films can help you plan effectively. Typically, the journey from initial contact to final delivery involves several key phases, which we'll outline here.

1. Initial Consultation (1-2 Weeks)

The first step begins with an initial consultation where you discuss your vision, objectives, and potential content for the video. This phase is crucial as it allows Edge of Story Films to understand your specific needs and the message you want to convey. During this time, you can expect to share details about your business, including any unique aspects of your South Weymouth location that you want to highlight, such as your proximity to local landmarks like the Shea Naval Aviation Museum.

2. Concept Development (2-3 Weeks)

Once the consultation is complete, the next phase is concept development. Edge of Story Films will take the insights gathered and develop a creative concept tailored to your goals. This may involve scripting, storyboarding, and presenting ideas for visual elements that resonate with your audience. At this stage, you’ll also discuss logistics, such as locations for filming, which might include notable spots in South Weymouth that align with your brand. This phase typically lasts 2-3 weeks, depending on the complexity of the project and your feedback.

3. Production (1-3 Weeks)

Following concept approval, the production phase begins. This is where the filming takes place, which can take anywhere from one to three weeks based on factors like the length of the video, the number of locations, and the availability of your team for interviews. Edge of Story Films uses high-definition cameras for a polished finish, ensuring that your corporate video reflects your brand’s professionalism. It's important to have key team members available during this time, so planning ahead is crucial.

4. Post-Production (2-4 Weeks)

Next comes the post-production phase, which entails editing the footage, adding motion graphics, sound design, and any necessary animations. This phase usually takes 2-4 weeks. Throughout this process, you’ll receive drafts to review, ensuring that the final product aligns with your vision. Clear communication during this phase helps prevent delays and ensures that your feedback is integrated smoothly.

5. Final Delivery (1 Week)

Finally, after revisions and approvals, the completed video is delivered. Edge of Story Films provides the final media in your desired formats, ready for distribution across your website and social media platforms. This last step typically takes about a week. It’s the culmination of a collaborative process aimed at creating a compelling video that effectively represents your brand.

Key Considerations

  • Timelines: While the overall timeline can range from approximately 6 to 12 weeks, various factors can influence this, such as scheduling conflicts, the complexity of the video, and approval cycles.
  • Clear Communication: Maintaining open lines of communication with Edge of Story Films will help streamline the process and minimize potential delays.
