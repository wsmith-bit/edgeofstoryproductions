1. Check Credentials and Experience

When evaluating potential video production companies, start by looking at their credentials. Ensure they have experience working with businesses similar to yours, particularly in your industry. Ask about their previous projects and clients to gauge their expertise. For instance, if you're with a law firm or a B2B tech startup, inquire if they have produced attorney intro videos or animated explainers specific to your sector.

2. Review Their Portfolio

A strong portfolio is a significant indicator of a video production company’s capabilities. Look for examples of their previous work that align with your vision. Edge of Story Films showcases various styles, from corporate video production to marketing videos. Pay attention to:

  • Quality: Are the videos high-definition and professionally edited?
  • Diversity: Do they offer a range of video types that meet your needs?
  • Client Testimonials: Are there reviews or case studies available that highlight their success?

3. Ask the Right Questions

During your initial conversations, be prepared to ask specific questions to assess the company's fit for your project. Consider asking:

  • What is your process for concept development and production?
  • How do you ensure that the final product aligns with my goals?
  • What is your typical timeline for completing a project?
  • Can you provide case studies or examples of measurable success?

4. Identify Red Flags

While evaluating potential companies, be aware of any red flags that might suggest a lack of professionalism or capability. Some warning signs include:

  • Inconsistent quality in their portfolio or demo reel.
  • Lack of transparency about pricing or production timelines.
  • Poor communication during initial conversations, which may indicate future issues.
