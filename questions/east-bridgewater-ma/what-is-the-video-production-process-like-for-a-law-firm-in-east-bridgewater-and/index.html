Understanding the Video Production Process for East Bridgewater Law Firms

When a law firm in East Bridgewater decides to invest in professional video production, understanding the process from start to finish is crucial. At Edge of Story Films, we provide a streamlined video production experience tailored for legal professionals. Our goal is to create engaging and credible video content that effectively communicates your firm’s unique value proposition to potential clients.

1. Initial Consultation

The first step in the process is an initial consultation, where we discuss your firm's specific goals, target audience, and type of videos needed—such as attorney introduction videos or client testimonials. This meeting typically takes place within a week of your inquiry and can be conducted at your office near East Bridgewater Town Common or via video call.

2. Concept Development

After the initial consultation, our team will develop a creative concept tailored to your needs. This phase usually takes about 1-2 weeks, during which we will provide a detailed plan outlining the video’s message, style, and any necessary elements such as scripts or storyboards. We encourage your team to share any specific ideas or preferences you might have.

3. Pre-Production Planning

Once the concept is approved, we move into pre-production. This phase includes scheduling the shoot, securing locations (which could be at your East Bridgewater office), and arranging any necessary talent or crew. Depending on your availability and the complexity of the shoot, this can take 1-3 weeks. It’s important to keep in mind that scheduling can take longer if your attorneys have limited availability due to case commitments.

4. Filming

Filming usually takes 1-2 days, depending on the number of videos being produced. Our team will handle everything from camera setup to directing on-site, helping your attorneys feel comfortable in front of the camera. We shoot in high-definition formats (4K and 6K) to ensure your videos maintain a professional quality that resonates with potential clients.

5. Post-Production

Following the filming, we enter the post-production phase, which typically lasts about 2-4 weeks. This is where we edit the footage, add motion graphics, animations, and any other necessary elements to create a polished final product. We’ll share drafts with your team during this phase to gather feedback and make adjustments as needed.

6. Final Delivery

Once the editing is complete, we deliver the final videos in the desired formats, typically within 1 week after the last round of edits. You’ll receive high-quality files suitable for your website, social media channels, and other marketing efforts.

Potential Delays and a Smooth Experience

  • Delays: Scheduling conflicts with attorneys, unforeseen changes in your firm’s marketing strategy, or extended feedback cycles can impact the timeline.
  • Smooth Experience: To ensure a seamless process, maintain open communication with our team and have key decision-makers available during the consultation and feedback phases.
