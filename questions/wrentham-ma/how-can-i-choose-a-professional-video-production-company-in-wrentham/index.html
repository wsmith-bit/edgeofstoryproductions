Questions to Ask on the First Call

  • What is your approach to developing a video content strategy that aligns with my business goals?
  • Can you walk me through your process from concept development to final delivery?
  • How do you ensure the final product reflects my brand’s identity and values?
  • What is your experience with clients in my industry, and can you share relevant case studies?

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Generic Responses: If the provider offers cookie-cutter solutions without taking the time to understand your specific needs or objectives, it may indicate a lack of strategic focus.
  • Poor Communication: If the initial communication is slow or unprofessional, this may signal how they will handle your project.
  • Lack of Transparent Pricing: Be cautious of providers who are vague about costs or have hidden fees. A reputable company should provide a clear breakdown of what services are included.
