Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

If you're a business in Auburn, Massachusetts, seeking to create a professional corporate video, understanding the process and timeline with Edge of Story Films will help you prepare for a smooth collaboration. From initial contact to final delivery, here’s what to expect.

1. Initial Contact and Consultation

The process begins with reaching out to Edge of Story Films, either through their website or by calling (781) 486-3565. During this initial contact, you will discuss your project requirements, such as the type of video you need—be it a corporate video, testimonial, or promotional content. This phase typically lasts about 1 week, depending on your availability for a consultation.

2. Creative Development

Once you’ve shared your vision, the team will move into the creative development phase, which usually takes 1-2 weeks. This involves brainstorming ideas, drafting scripts, and developing storyboards. Edge of Story Films focuses on understanding your brand and audience, ensuring the final product aligns with your marketing goals. For instance, if you're a local healthcare facility in Auburn, they will tailor your video to resonate with the specific demographics and needs of the community.

3. Pre-Production Planning

Following the script finalization, the pre-production phase kicks off. This includes scheduling filming dates, securing locations (which may include Auburn landmarks like the Auburn Cord Duesenberg Automobile Museum), and coordinating any necessary talent or crew. This stage can take around 1 week, and clear communication is vital to avoid scheduling delays, especially if multiple stakeholders—like attorneys or marketing directors—are involved.

4. Filming

The actual filming typically occurs over 1-3 days, depending on the complexity of the video. Edge of Story Films utilizes high-definition 4K and 6K cameras to ensure the best quality. During this phase, you can expect a collaborative effort where the team guides on-camera talent, helping to humanize the message and present a polished final product.

5. Post-Production

After filming, the project shifts to post-production, which generally takes 2-3 weeks. This includes editing the footage, adding motion graphics, sound design, and any necessary revisions based on your feedback. Edge of Story Films places a strong emphasis on delivering a video that meets your strategic needs, so be prepared to provide input during this stage.

6. Final Delivery

The final video will be delivered in your preferred format, ready for use on your website, social media, or other platforms. This is typically completed within 1 week after the edits are finalized. A seamless experience at this stage relies on timely feedback and approvals from your team.

Potential Delays

  • Availability of key personnel for filming and approvals
  • Changes in project scope or direction after the initial consultation
  • Compliance review processes, especially for industries like financial services

Conclusion

In summary, the entire process from initial contact to final delivery can take approximately 6-10 weeks, depending on the complexity of the project and responsiveness from your team.
