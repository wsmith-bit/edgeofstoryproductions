Understanding the Process and Timeline for Corporate Video Production in West Newton

If you're considering a corporate video project in West Newton, working with Edge of Story Films can streamline the process and ensure a high-quality product. Knowing what to expect at each stage will help you navigate the production smoothly and efficiently.

Phase 1: Initial Consultation (1-2 weeks)

  • Discovery Meeting: Start by reaching out to Edge of Story Films to schedule a discovery meeting. In this meeting, you'll discuss your goals, audience, and the message you want to convey.
  • Proposal and Agreement: After the initial consultation, Edge of Story Films will draft a proposal outlining the project scope, timeline, and budget. Expect to review and finalize the agreement within 1-2 weeks.

Phase 2: Pre-Production (2-4 weeks)

  • Creative Development: Once the agreement is signed, the team will work on developing the creative concept. This includes storyboarding, scripting, and planning the logistics of the shoot.
  • Scheduling: Edge of Story Films will coordinate with you to schedule filming dates, accommodating your team’s availability. Keep in mind that scheduling can be affected by factors such as your firm's workload and the availability of key personnel.
  • Location Scouting: If filming on location, Edge of Story Films will scout and finalize the locations, ensuring they align with your project’s vision. For West Newton clients, this might include local landmarks or your office space.

Phase 3: Production (1-3 days)

  • Shooting: The filming typically takes 1-3 days, depending on the complexity of the project. Edge of Story Films will handle all technical aspects, allowing you to focus on delivering your message.
  • On-Site Direction: The team will provide direction to ensure that everyone involved feels comfortable in front of the camera, which is especially important for professionals who may be camera-shy.

Phase 4: Post-Production (2-4 weeks)

  • Editing: Once filming is complete, the editing process begins. Edge of Story Films will compile the footage, add graphics, and create a polished final product. This phase usually takes 2-4 weeks.
  • Client Review: You will receive a draft for review. This is your opportunity to provide feedback and request revisions, ensuring the final product meets your expectations.

Phase 5: Final Delivery (1 week)

  • Final Edits: After incorporating your feedback, Edge of Story Films will finalize the video and deliver it in your preferred format.
  • Distribution Guidance: They can also provide recommendations on how to effectively distribute your video, whether through your website, social media, or other channels.

Potential Delays

While the timeline provides a general overview, delays can occur, particularly during the review phases or if scheduling conflicts arise. To facilitate a smooth experience, it’s crucial to communicate clearly and promptly with the Edge of Story Films team throughout the process.

Conclusion

In conclusion, collaborating with Edge of Story Films for your corporate video project in West Newton is a structured process that emphasizes professionalism and quality. By understanding the phases from consultation to final delivery, you can set realistic expectations and ensure a successful outcome.
