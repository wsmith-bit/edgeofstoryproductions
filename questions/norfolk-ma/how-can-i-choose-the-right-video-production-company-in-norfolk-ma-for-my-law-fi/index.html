Choosing the Right Video Production Company in Norfolk, MA

When searching for a video production company for your law firm in Norfolk, MA, it’s essential to evaluate potential providers carefully. A well-crafted video can build trust, humanize your attorneys, and ultimately drive qualified leads to your practice. Considering the unique landscape of Norfolk, with its population of over 11,600 and a mix of local businesses, finding the right fit for your firm's needs is vital.

Key Criteria to Consider

  • Credentials and Experience: Look for a company that has a strong portfolio and extensive experience in legal video production. Check if they have worked with firms similar to yours in size and practice area. Edge of Story Films, based in nearby Wakefield, has a reputation for working with law firms in the Greater Boston area, making them a relevant choice.
  • Portfolio Signals: Review their previous work to assess the quality and style of their videos. Pay attention to how they present attorneys and legal concepts. A good video production company should showcase attorney intro videos, client testimonials, and website videos that resonate with your prospective clients.
  • Questions to Ask: When you first contact a potential provider, ask specific questions to gauge their understanding of your industry. For example:
    • Can you share examples of how you’ve helped law firms build credibility through video?
    • What is your process for making attorneys comfortable on camera?
    • How do you ensure the final product aligns with our brand and complies with legal advertising standards?
  • Red Flags to Watch For: Be cautious of providers who lack experience in legal video production or cannot provide testimonials or case studies. If they can't demonstrate a clear understanding of the legal market or if their previous work looks cheap or unprofessional, consider this a warning sign.
