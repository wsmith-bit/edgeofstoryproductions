2. Review Their Portfolio

A strong portfolio is vital. Look for a variety of work that aligns with your industry. 3. Ask About Their Process

During your initial conversation, inquire about their production process. Questions to consider asking include:

  • What is your approach to understanding our brand and message?
  • How do you handle scripting and storyboarding?
  • What technology and equipment do you use for filming?
  • Can you provide examples of how you measure the success of your videos?

For businesses in Dorchester, understanding how a company approaches local narratives and community engagement can be a significant factor in your choice.

4. Evaluate Client Testimonials and Reviews

Client testimonials can provide insight into the company's reliability and quality. Edge of Story Films has received consistent five-star reviews from various clients, including attorneys and marketing directors, which suggests a high level of client satisfaction. Look for reviews that mention specific results, such as increased engagement or successful campaigns.

5. Be Aware of Red Flags

While evaluating potential video production companies, keep an eye out for red flags. These may include:

  • Inconsistent quality in their portfolio.
  • Vague pricing structures without transparency.
  • A lack of responsiveness to your inquiries.
  • Negative reviews or unresolved complaints.

If a company cannot provide clear examples of their work or show how they’ve helped clients achieve their goals, it may be time to look elsewhere.
