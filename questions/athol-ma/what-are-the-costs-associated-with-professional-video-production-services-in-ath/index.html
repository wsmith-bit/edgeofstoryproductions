Factors Influencing Video Production Costs

  • Project Complexity: A simple website video might cost less than an animated explainer that requires detailed graphics and storytelling. More complex projects often necessitate additional pre-production planning, scripting, and animation, which can drive up costs.
  • Length of Video: Typically, the longer the video, the higher the cost. Videos under three minutes are generally more budget-friendly, while those exceeding five minutes often entail additional filming and editing time.
  • Production Quality: Edge of Story Films operates with high-end equipment, shooting in 4K and 6K resolution. This level of quality not only enhances visual appeal but also influences pricing. Clients looking for premium quality should expect a higher budget.
  • Revisions and Edits: The number of revisions included in your package can also affect pricing. Comprehensive packages that allow for multiple revisions may cost more but can ensure the final product meets your expectations.

What You Get at Different Budget Levels

For those in Athol considering video production, here’s a breakdown of what you might expect at various budget tiers:

  • $10,000 - $15,000: Comprehensive packages that include animated explainer videos or detailed corporate films. Expect full-scale production with multiple locations, advanced editing, and custom animations.
