Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

When a law firm in Ware, Massachusetts, decides to engage with Edge of Story Films for professional video production, they can expect a structured and collaborative process aimed at creating impactful video content. This process is designed to address the specific needs of law firms, such as building credibility, humanizing attorneys, and effectively communicating complex legal concepts.

Step 1: Initial Consultation

The journey begins with an initial consultation between the law firm and Edge of Story Films. During this meeting, the team will discuss the firm’s goals, target audience, and specific video needs—such as attorney intro videos or client testimonials. This usually takes about one week to schedule, considering the busy schedules of managing partners and marketing directors.

Step 2: Creative Concept Development

After understanding the law firm’s objectives, Edge of Story Films will move into the creative concept development phase. In this one to two-week period, the team will outline the video’s narrative and decide on the style and format that best fits the firm’s brand. For example, a firm in Ware may want to showcase its community involvement or highlight successful case outcomes to build trust with potential clients.

Step 3: Pre-Production Planning

Next, the pre-production phase begins, spanning approximately two to three weeks. This involves scripting, storyboarding, and scheduling shoots. The law firm will need to prepare by gathering necessary materials, like client testimonials and relevant case studies. It is also essential to coordinate filming schedules, especially considering attorneys' time constraints. A smooth pre-production phase can significantly enhance the overall timeline.

Step 4: Filming

Filming typically takes one to three days, depending on the scale of the project. Edge of Story Films will handle all aspects of filming, including directing attorneys who may be camera-shy. Their experience ensures that the video feels authentic and engaging. Ware's local landmarks, like Veterans' Park, can be incorporated into the filming to give a sense of community and connection.

Step 5: Post-Production

Once filming is complete, the project moves into the post-production phase, which usually lasts about two to four weeks. This includes editing, adding motion graphics, and sound design. The law firm can expect to receive drafts for review and feedback during this stage. Quick responses from the firm can help maintain the timeline, as edits may require several rounds of feedback.

Step 6: Final Delivery

After incorporating feedback, Edge of Story Films will deliver the final video files. This process can take an additional week, allowing for any last-minute adjustments. The law firm can then utilize the video across their website and social media platforms, driving traffic and engagement from prospective clients in Ware and beyond.

Summary

Overall, the entire video production process can take approximately eight to twelve weeks from initial consultation to final delivery, depending on the firm’s responsiveness and the complexity of the project. By partnering with Edge of Story Films, law firms in Ware can create high-quality video content that enhances their online presence and effectively communicates their unique value proposition.
