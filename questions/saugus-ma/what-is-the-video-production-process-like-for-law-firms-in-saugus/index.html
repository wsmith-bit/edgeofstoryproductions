Understanding the Video Production Process for Law Firms in Saugus

If you're a law firm in Saugus looking to enhance your online presence and build credibility with prospective clients, understanding the video production process is essential. At Edge of Story Films, we guide you through each step to ensure a smooth and efficient experience from initial contact to final delivery.

Initial Consultation

Once you reach out to us, we will schedule an initial consultation to discuss your specific needs. During this phase, you'll have the opportunity to share your vision, goals, and any challenges you've faced with previous video content. This meeting typically lasts about an hour. We aim to understand your firm's practice areas—be it personal injury, corporate, or family law—and how we can best showcase your unique value in the Saugus market.

Concept Development

After our consultation, we move into the concept development phase. We'll present you with a tailored proposal outlining the types of videos we recommend, such as attorney introduction videos or client testimonials, and how they can address your goals. This phase usually takes about one to two weeks, during which we may refine the concept based on your feedback.

Scripting and Storyboarding

Once the concept is approved, we will create a script and storyboard. This step is crucial as it lays the foundation for the visual narrative. The scripting process can take another week, as we collaborate closely with your team to ensure the messaging aligns with your firm's values and services. In Saugus, where many law firms are competing for visibility, having a clear and engaging story is vital.

Filming

Next, we schedule the filming. This is where the magic happens! We handle all aspects of production, ensuring that the shoot captures high-quality footage that resonates with your audience. Typically, filming can be completed in one to two days, depending on the number of videos we are producing. We work around your schedule to minimize disruptions, particularly since many attorneys are busy and difficult to schedule.

Editing and Post-Production

After filming, our editing team gets to work, crafting the footage into polished, professional videos. This phase includes adding motion graphics, animations, and sound design. The editing process generally takes two to three weeks. Should any delays occur—like additional feedback or compliance checks—we will communicate proactively to keep you informed.

Final Review and Delivery

After editing, we will present you with the final videos for review. This is your opportunity to request any changes or adjustments before we deliver the final product. Once approved, we will provide you with the videos in the formats you need for your website, social media, or other platforms. The entire process, from initial contact to final delivery, typically takes about 6 to 8 weeks, depending on the complexity of the project and your availability.
