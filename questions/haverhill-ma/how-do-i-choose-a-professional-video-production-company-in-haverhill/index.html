Key Criteria for Evaluation

  • Experience Signals: Seek out companies that have worked with clients in your industry. For example, if you run a law firm in Haverhill, a provider who has experience creating attorney intro videos and client testimonials will be better suited to understand your needs.
  • Portfolio Markers: Review the company's portfolio for diversity in projects. Look for high-quality production values, creativity, and a range of services offered. Ensure they can provide content shot in 4K or 6K resolution, which is crucial for high-end marketing materials.

Questions to Ask on the First Call

  • What is your approach to understanding our brand and target audience?
  • Can you share case studies or examples of similar projects you've completed?
  • What is your production timeline, and how do you manage revisions?
  • How do you handle compliance issues, especially for financial services or legal content?

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Lack of Transparency: Be wary of providers who are not willing to share their previous work or client testimonials. Transparency is key to building trust.
  • Inconsistent Quality: If the portfolio shows a wide variation in quality across projects, it may indicate that the company lacks a standardized approach to production.
  • High Turnover: Frequent changes in team members or leadership can affect the quality and consistency of the final product.
