Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

When considering professional video production services for your law firm in Jamaica Plain, it's essential to understand the steps involved from initial contact to final delivery. Edge of Story Films specializes in crafting tailored video content that helps law firms enhance their online presence and build trust with prospective clients. Here’s a detailed breakdown of what to expect throughout the process.

Step 1: Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with an initial consultation. During this phase, you’ll meet with a representative from Edge of Story Films to discuss your specific needs. This typically takes about 1 week to schedule, depending on availability. Be prepared to share your firm’s goals, target audience, and any previous video content that you felt was lacking. This will help the team understand your brand and the message you want to convey.

Step 2: Creative Concept Development

Following the consultation, the team will move into the creative concept development phase, which usually lasts about 2-3 weeks. Here, they will brainstorm and present ideas for your video content, whether it’s an attorney introduction, client testimonials, or case update explainers. You will have the opportunity to provide feedback and finalize the concepts, ensuring they align with your firm’s vision and branding.

Step 3: Pre-Production Planning

Once the concepts are approved, the Edge of Story Films team will dive into pre-production planning, taking about 2 weeks. This includes scripting, storyboarding, and scheduling shoot dates. If you have specific locations in Jamaica Plain, such as a local landmark like the Franklin Park Olmsted Park, where you want to film, now is the time to communicate that. They will also coordinate with you to ensure the attorneys who will be on camera are available.

Step 4: Filming

The filming phase is usually completed in 1-2 days, depending on the complexity of the project. Edge of Story Films employs high-definition cameras to capture your firm's story authentically. Their crew will guide your attorneys to ensure they feel comfortable and natural on camera, addressing any concerns about appearing stiff or unnatural. This is particularly important for law firms, as humanizing your attorneys can lead to stronger connections with potential clients.

Step 5: Post-Production

After filming, the post-production phase begins, taking approximately 3-4 weeks. This involves editing the footage, adding motion graphics, and incorporating any necessary animations. Edge of Story Films prioritizes delivering a polished final product that resonates with your audience and reflects your brand’s professionalism. Throughout this phase, they will share drafts with you, allowing for any adjustments before finalizing the video.

Step 6: Final Review and Delivery

Once the video is complete, you’ll have the opportunity for a final review. This process typically takes about 1 week. After you provide your approval, they will deliver the final video file in your preferred format. You can then utilize this video on your website, social media channels, or during client presentations, maximizing its reach and impact.

Potential Delays

While the outlined timeline provides a good overview, several factors can cause delays. For instance, scheduling conflicts with your attorneys or feedback rounds that take longer than anticipated can extend the timeline. However, clear communication and prompt feedback can help keep everything on track.

Conclusion

In summary, the video production process with Edge of Story Films is designed to be collaborative and efficient, ensuring that your law firm in Jamaica Plain receives a high-quality video that meets your specific needs.
