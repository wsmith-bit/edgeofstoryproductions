Understanding the Video Production Process for Law Firms in Littleton

If you are a managing partner or marketing director of a law firm in Littleton, Massachusetts, you may be considering professional video production services to enhance your online presence and build trust with prospective clients. At Edge of Story Films, we offer end-to-end video production tailored specifically for law firms, ensuring that your firm stands out in a competitive market. Here’s what you can expect from our process, from initial contact through to final delivery.

1. Initial Consultation (1 Week)

Once you reach out to us via phone or our website, we will schedule a consultation to discuss your specific needs. This step typically takes about a week. We will explore your goals, such as humanizing your attorneys or showcasing client testimonials to build credibility. During this meeting, we’ll also discuss your firm’s unique selling points and the specific type of video content you require, be it an attorney introduction video or a case update explainer.

2. Concept Development (2 Weeks)

After our initial consultation, we will work on developing a creative concept that aligns with your objectives. This phase usually takes about two weeks. We will create a storyboard or outline for your video, incorporating your firm’s values and services. This step is crucial in ensuring that the final product effectively communicates your message to potential clients in Littleton, particularly given the local industry compliance and credentialing requirements.

3. Filming (1-2 Weeks)

Once the concept is approved, we will schedule a filming date. Depending on the complexity of the shoot, this could take one to two weeks. We understand that attorneys are often busy, so we will work with you to find suitable times for filming. Our team will ensure a smooth filming process, utilizing high-definition cameras to capture your firm’s story authentically. We often film at your office in Littleton or at relevant local landmarks to create a relatable connection with your audience.

4. Post-Production (2-4 Weeks)

After filming, our editing team will begin the post-production phase, which typically takes between two to four weeks. During this phase, we will edit the footage, add motion graphics, and ensure that the final product reflects your brand’s voice and meets compliance requirements. We’ll also incorporate feedback from your team to ensure the video aligns with your expectations.

5. Final Review and Delivery (1 Week)

Once editing is complete, we will send you a draft for review. This is your opportunity to provide any final feedback or request adjustments. After receiving your approval, we will deliver the final video, typically within a week. The final product will be available in multiple formats for your website, social media, and other marketing channels.

Potential Delays

  • Scheduling Conflicts: Coordinating schedules with busy attorneys can lead to potential delays in the filming phase.
  • Feedback Loops: Timely feedback during the post-production phase can expedite the delivery process.

By understanding this video production process, law firms in Littleton can effectively plan their projects to fit within their marketing timelines.
