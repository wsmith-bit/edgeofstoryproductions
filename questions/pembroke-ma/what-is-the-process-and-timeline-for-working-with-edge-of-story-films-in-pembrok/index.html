Understanding the Process and Timeline for Corporate Video Production in Pembroke

If you're a business in Pembroke looking to create a professional corporate video, working with Edge of Story Films is a streamlined process that ensures quality and effectiveness. From the moment you reach out, our team will guide you through every step, helping to produce a video that aligns with your goals and resonates with your audience.

Step 1: Initial Consultation (1 Week)

The process begins with an initial consultation. During this stage, you’ll discuss your specific objectives, whether it's to enhance your online presence, humanize your brand, or provide clarity on complex services. This meeting typically takes place over the phone or via video call, and it helps us understand your vision.

Step 2: Concept Development (1-2 Weeks)

After the consultation, our creative team will develop a concept tailored to your needs. This phase involves brainstorming ideas, scripting, and storyboarding. You'll receive an outline to review, and feedback is encouraged to ensure we are on the right track. Depending on your promptness in providing feedback, this phase can take between one to two weeks.

Step 3: Pre-Production Planning (2 Weeks)

Once the concept is approved, we move into pre-production. This includes scheduling filming dates, securing locations (which could range from your Pembroke office to Herring Run Historical Park), and coordinating talent. It’s essential to finalize logistics to avoid any delays. Typically, this stage lasts about two weeks, but factors such as actor availability or location permissions may extend this timeline.

Step 4: Filming (1-3 Days)

Filming generally takes place over one to three days, depending on the complexity of the project. Our team shoots in high-definition 4K or 6K quality, ensuring your final product is visually stunning. If your Pembroke business requires multiple locations or intricate scenes, the filming phase may take longer. However, our efficient crew works diligently to stay on schedule.

Step 5: Post-Production (2-4 Weeks)

Post-production is where the magic happens. This phase includes editing, sound design, and adding any necessary graphics or animations. Edge of Story Films typically delivers a first cut within two weeks, allowing you to provide feedback. The revision process can take an additional two weeks, depending on the complexity of your requests and how quickly feedback is given.

Step 6: Final Delivery (1 Week)

After revisions are complete, the final product will be delivered digitally. This can include various formats suitable for your website, social media, or corporate presentations. The final delivery typically occurs within one week after the last round of edits is approved.

What Could Cause Delays?

  • Feedback Timeliness: Delays in your feedback during the consultation, concept development, or post-production stages can push back the timeline.
  • Logistics Issues: Challenges in securing locations or coordinating schedules can also affect the pre-production timeline.
  • Complexity of Edits: If more significant changes are requested during post-production, this could extend the editing timeline.

A Smooth Experience

To ensure a smooth experience, clear communication and prompt feedback on your part are crucial. Our team is committed to keeping you informed at every stage, making sure the final product meets your expectations and fulfills your objectives.
