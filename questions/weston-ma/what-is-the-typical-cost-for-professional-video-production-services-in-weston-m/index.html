Factors Influencing Cost

  • Type of Video: Different video types come with varying production demands. For instance, a simple corporate video or about-us video may start around $1,500 to $3,000. In contrast, a more complex animated explainer video or a series of testimonials could escalate costs to between $5,000 and $10,000.
  • Production Quality: Edge of Story Films prides itself on delivering high-definition video in 4K and 6K resolution, which typically incurs higher costs due to advanced equipment and expertise. Lower-quality production options can be cheaper but may not provide the same level of impact.
  • Project Duration: The length of the video and the overall production timeline also play a crucial role. Short videos with minimal edits may cost less, while longer, more detailed projects with extensive editing, motion graphics, and animations will require more resources and time, driving up costs.
  • Location and Logistics: Weston’s affluent nature and proximity to Boston mean that clients often seek high-quality production that stands out. Edge of Story Films' experience with local businesses ensures they understand the unique demands and expectations of the Weston market.
