Understanding the Video Production Process with Edge of Story Films

If you’re a business or organization in Spencer, MA, looking to create professional video content, understanding the video production process with Edge of Story Films is essential. The process is streamlined and designed to deliver high-quality results that effectively communicate your message. Here’s a detailed overview of what you can expect from first contact through to final delivery.

Step 1: Initial Consultation

The process begins with an initial consultation, which typically occurs within a week of your inquiry. During this meeting, you’ll discuss your project’s goals, target audience, and specific video needs. Whether you’re looking for testimonials, corporate videos, or animated explainers, this is the time to share your vision. The team will ask questions to understand your brand and the message you want to convey.

Step 2: Concept Development

Once the consultation is complete, the Edge of Story Films team will develop a creative concept tailored to your needs. This phase usually takes about 1-2 weeks. It involves brainstorming ideas, drafting a script, and outlining the visual style of the video. You will be involved in this process, providing feedback and ensuring the concept aligns with your objectives.

Step 3: Pre-Production Planning

The pre-production phase is crucial and can take 2-4 weeks. This stage includes setting a shooting schedule, securing locations, and arranging any necessary permits, especially pertinent in a town like Spencer, where local regulations may apply. You will also finalize the script and review any logistics, such as talent and equipment needed for the shoot.

Step 4: Production

Production is where the magic happens! Typically, this phase lasts 1-3 days depending on the complexity of your project. Edge of Story Films utilizes high-definition 4K or 6K cameras to ensure stunning visuals. Filming will take place at designated locations, which could include local landmarks in Spencer such as the Central Museum or Prouty Park, adding a local touch to your video.

Step 5: Post-Production

Post-production follows filming and usually lasts 2-4 weeks. This is where the editing, motion graphics, and sound design come into play. The team will compile the footage, create a cohesive narrative, and add any necessary graphics or animations. You will receive a rough cut for review, allowing you to provide input before the final version is completed.

Step 6: Delivery

After final adjustments based on your feedback, the completed video will be delivered in your preferred format. This delivery phase can take about a week. Edge of Story Films ensures that your video is optimized for various platforms, whether it’s for social media, your website, or an internal training session.

What Could Cause Delays?

  • Inadequate preparation or slow feedback during the initial stages.
  • Logistical challenges such as weather conditions or location issues.
  • Changes in project scope or last-minute alterations to the script.

A Smooth Experience

A smooth experience hinges on clear communication and timely feedback at each stage. By being proactive and engaged, you can help ensure that the project stays on schedule and meets your expectations.
