📊 VIDEO PRODUCTION IN COMPARISON TO VIDEOGRAPHY

VIDEO PRODUCTION

What it is

Strategy from start to finish:concept development, scripting, shooting, editing, motion graphics, audio, distribution.

Team

Director, producer, director of photography, lighting technician, sound engineer, editor, colorist, motion graphics designer.

Best for

Product launches, brand storytelling, commercials, client testimonials, explainers, recruitment, thought leadership.

We are a full-service video production agency serving B2B companies.

Our Process:Discovery, narrative development, production, post-production, and distribution.

Our Outcomes:Clear communication, brand-aligned visuals, and quantifiable outcomes.

VIDEOGRAPHY

What it is

Independent, less experienced videographer concentrating on capturing moments with minimal editing.

Team

Single videographer.

Best for

Weddings, graduations, live performances, recitals, and local sporting events.

Our primary focus is on B2B video production. We create event videos for B2B clients, but we do not cover wedding videography.

If you need event-style videography, we can help determine the best approach or suggest a trusted specialist.
