📊 VIDEO PRODUCTION VS. VIDEOGRAPHY

VIDEO PRODUCTION

What it is

From conceptualization to delivery:idea generation, scriptwriting, filming, editing, motion graphics, audio, distribution.

Team

Director, producer, cinematographer, lighting technician, sound engineer, editor, colorist, motion graphics artist.

Best for

Product launches, brand stories, commercials, client testimonials, explainer videos, recruitment, thought leadership content.

We are a full-service video production company serving B2B clients.

Our Process:Research, storytelling, production, post-production, and distribution.

Our Outcomes:Clear communication, brand-consistent visuals, and measurable outcomes.

VIDEOGRAPHY

What it is

An independent, less experienced videographer focused on capturing moments with minimal editing.

Team

Single videographer.

Best for

Weddings, graduations, live events, recitals, community sports events.

Our primary focus is on B2B video production. We create event videos for B2B clients but do not provide wedding videography.

If you need event-style videography, we can suggest the best strategy or recommend a trusted professional.
