📊 VIDEO PRODUCTION VS. VIDEOGRAPHY

VIDEO PRODUCTION

What it is

Strategy from inception to completion:idea development, scripting, shooting, editing, motion graphics, audio, and distribution.

Team

Director, producer, cinematographer, lighting technician, sound engineer, editor, colorist, motion graphics designer.

Best for

Product launches, branding films, advertisements, customer testimonials, explainers, recruitment, thought leadership content.

We are a full-service video production agency serving B2B organizations.

Our Process:Discovery, storytelling, production, post-production, and distribution.

Our Outcomes:Clear communication, brand-appropriate visuals, and quantifiable results.

VIDEOGRAPHY

What it is

Independent, less seasoned videographer focusing on recording events with minimal editing.

Team

Single videographer.

Best for

Weddings, graduations, performances, recitals, and local sports events.

Our main focus is B2B video production. We do create event videos for B2B clients but we do not cover weddings.

Should you need event-style videography, we can outline an appropriate approach or recommend a trusted professional.
